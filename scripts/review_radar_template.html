Review Radar

Where the text alone would land

Three priors computed from the abstract before any reviewer sees the evidence. They say what the topic and framing buy on their own; the GPU results are what move a paper off these numbers.

The shape a paper needs

Sub-scores of accepted versus rejected neighbours, averaged over their reviewers. The gap is where effort pays.

What we need to do, from the comparables' reviews

G1
Scale and leaderboards. Every accepted comparable had many datasets and several model families; the rejected ones were thin. Full GIFT-Eval and fev-bench through the official harnesses, four oracle families including a current state-of-the-art one, a compute table.
G2
The baselines reviewers asked the comparables for. Best fixed context, TATO-style transformation search, test-time adaptation (COSA, TAFAS), a conformally recalibrated oracle, SFT of the oracle at matched compute. ZooCast was rejected because its combination lost to the best single model; our regret claim has to be shown, not proved.
G3
Reproduce before you refute. Run each audited recipe from its released code, replicate its reported point gains, then show the coverage cost with intervals. Otherwise "straw man".
G4
Cross-oracle transfer as a headline. No comparable has it; it is the one novelty lever that is ours alone.
G5
Presentation. Four of five accepted comparables took a presentation score of 2 from someone; a rating-2 review led with "deficient writing". One figure per claim, tables that fit, no red TODOs.
G6
Answer "why RL at all" in the paper. Either a policy-gradient variant beats exact online learning on the discrete decision, or the audit carries the RL claim and the title says so.
